# Fully Funded Undergraduate Aerospace Engineering Shortlist

**Target Audience:** Indian High School Applicants (Class 11/12)  
**Budget Constraint:** Under ₹2 Lakhs INR per year total out-of-pocket cost  
**Degree Target:** Aeronautical / Aerospace Engineering (or Mechanical with Aerospace specialization)  
**Language Limit:** English or Max B2 Italian  

---

## 1. Strategy & Budget Realignment Overview

Living expenses (housing, food, insurance) in the US, UK, and Europe easily reach **10–15 Lakhs INR annually**. Therefore, tuition waivers alone are insufficient; **every university on this list must offer funding that covers both full tuition and living expenses (room and board)**.

### Exclusions & Removals

* **Georgia Tech, Purdue, Imperial College London:** Removed due to lack of comprehensive full-ride funding for international undergraduates.
* **University of Sheffield & University of Birmingham:** Removed because scholarships are partial (max 50% tuition), leaving >20 Lakhs INR/year in remaining tuition and UK living costs.
* **INSA Lyon & ISAE-SUPAERO (France):** Removed because the *Classes Préparatoires* entry pathway requires C1/C2 native-level French.
* **German Public Universities (TUM, RWTH, etc.):** Removed because undergraduate DAAD scholarships are non-existent, and the visa requires a Blocked Account containing ~€11,208 (~10 Lakhs INR) annually for living costs.

## 2. University Shortlist & Full-Ride Scholarship Breakdown

### Category A: USA — 100% Demonstrated Need & Merit Full Rides
*US private institutions evaluate family income via the CSS Profile. Families unable to contribute more than ₹2 Lakhs INR/year receive institutional grant aid covering all remaining costs.*

#### 1. Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT)
* **Program:** BS Aerospace Engineering
* **Scholarship:** MIT Financial Aid (Need-Blind)
* **Coverage:** Covers 100% of demonstrated financial need. Includes full tuition, on-campus housing, dining meal plans, health insurance, and an annual travel allowance to fly home to Pune. Out-of-pocket cost: $0.

#### 2. Stanford University
* **Program:** BS Aeronautics and Astronautics
* **Scholarship:** Stanford Financial Aid (Need-Aware)
* **Coverage:** Evaluates financial need during admission. Once admitted, Stanford covers 100% of demonstrated need: tuition, room, board, and books.

#### 3. Princeton University
* **Program:** BSE Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering
* **Scholarship:** Princeton Financial Aid (Need-Blind)
* **Coverage:** Grant-based aid with zero loan requirement. Covers full tuition, residential college room, dining, and personal expenses for eligible income brackets.

#### 4. California Institute of Technology (Caltech)
* **Program:** BS Aerospace
* **Scholarship:** Caltech Financial Aid (Need-Aware)
* **Coverage:** Caltech operates NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL). Meets 100% of demonstrated need for admitted international students (tuition, room, and board).

#### 5. Duke University
* **Program:** BS Mechanical Engineering (Aerospace Certificate)
* **Scholarship:** Karsh International Scholars Program (Merit-Based)
* **Coverage:** Full tuition, room, board, mandatory fees, plus dedicated funding for three summers of research, unpaid internships, or professional development.

#### 6. Harvard University
* **Program:** SB Engineering Sciences (Fluids/Aerospace Track)
* **Scholarship:** Harvard Financial Aid Initiative (Need-Blind)
* **Coverage:** Families earning under $85,000 USD (~₹70 Lakhs INR) pay nothing toward tuition, room, or board.

#### 7. Yale University
* **Program:** BS Mechanical Engineering (Aerospace Focus)
* **Scholarship:** Yale Financial Aid (Need-Blind)
* **Coverage:** Covers 100% of demonstrated financial need without loans, including full tuition, residential college housing, and meal plans.

#### 8. Dartmouth College
* **Program:** BA/BE Engineering (Mechanical/Aerospace Focus)
* **Scholarship:** Dartmouth Financial Aid (Need-Blind)
* **Coverage:** Need-blind for international applicants; meets 100% of demonstrated need for tuition, room, and board.

#### 9. Vanderbilt University
* **Program:** BE Mechanical Engineering (Aerospace Minor/Track)
* **Scholarship:** Chancellor's Scholarship + Need-Based Aid
* **Coverage:** Full tuition merit scholarship plus a summer research stipend. Demonstrated need funding covers remaining room and board.

#### 10. University of Notre Dame
* **Program:** BS Aerospace Engineering
* **Scholarship:** Stamps Scholars Program / Need-Based Aid
* **Coverage:** Meets 100% of demonstrated financial need. Stamps Scholars receive full tuition, room, board, and a $12,000 enrichment fund for research/travel.

### Category B: UK & Canada — Institutional Full Rides

#### 11. University of Oxford (UK)
* **Program:** MEng Engineering Science (Aerospace Specialization)
* **Scholarship:** Ellison Scholars Programme
* **Coverage:** 100% of course fees, a 52-week living stipend covering housing and food in Oxford, annual return flights to India, and a technology hardware grant.

#### 12. University of Toronto (Canada)
* **Program:** BASc Engineering Science (Aerospace Major)
* **Scholarship:** Lester B. Pearson International Scholarship
* **Coverage:** Full tuition, books, incidental fees, and full 4-year residence/meal plan support.  
* *Special Requirement:* Requires official nomination by your high school principal.

#### 13. University of British Columbia (Canada)
* **Program:** BASc Manufacturing/Mechanical Engineering (Aerospace Options)
* **Scholarship:** Karen McKellin International Leader of Tomorrow Award
* **Coverage:** Need-based award scaling to cover tuition, mandatory fees, and living costs minus family contribution (< ₹2 Lakhs INR).

### Category C: Continental Europe — DSU Regional State Funding

*Italian public university scholarships are granted via regional DSU (Diritto allo Studio Universitario) programs based on the family's financial profile (evaluated via ISEE Parificato).*

#### 14. Sapienza University of Rome (Italy)
* **Program:** BSc Aerospace Engineering (**Taught 100% in English**)
* **Scholarship:** LazioDiSco / DSU Regional Scholarship
* **Coverage:** 100% tuition waiver, free university dormitory housing, daily free canteen meals, and a yearly cash stipend (~€3,000–€7,000) for living expenses.

#### 15. Politecnico di Milano (PoliMi) (Italy)
* **Program:** BSc Aerospace Engineering (**Taught in Italian — Requires B2 Italian**)
* **Scholarship:** DSU PoliMi Regional Scholarship
* **Coverage:** 100% tuition waiver, priority free housing, daily canteen meals, and an annual stipend covering books and personal living costs.

### Category D: Asia & Russia — State & Institutional Quotas

#### 16. KAIST (South Korea)
* **Program:** BS Aerospace Engineering (**Taught 100% in English**)
* **Scholarship:** KAIST International Student Scholarship
* **Coverage:** 100% tuition coverage for 8 semesters, a monthly living allowance (~350,000 KRW / ~₹21,000 per month), and national health insurance.

#### 17. Moscow Aviation Institute (MAI) (Russia)
* **Program:** BSc Aeronautical Engineering (**Taught 100% in English**)
* **Scholarship:** Russian Government Scholarship Quota
* **Coverage:** 100% tuition waiver, placement in highly subsidized dormitories ($20–$50/month), and a monthly state stipend. Total out-of-pocket costs remain well under ₹2 Lakhs INR/year due to low living costs.

## 3. Vital Application Procedures

1. **Standardized Testing (US / Europe / Asia):**
   * Target **SAT score:** **1530–1560+** (Math: 790–800). Required/strongly recommended for MIT, Stanford, Caltech, Duke, KAIST, Sapienza, and PoliMi.
   * Target **IELTS/TOEFL:** IELTS 7.5+ (no band below 7.0) or TOEFL 100+.

2. **Financial Verification (CSS Profile & ISEE Parificato):**
   * **US & Canada:** Complete the **CSS Profile** in 12th grade using official Indian income tax returns (ITR) to document demonstrated need.
   * **Italy:** Obtain an **ISEE Parificato** certificate through an authorized CAF office/consulate to qualify for DSU full waivers and housing.

3. **Dummy School Coordination:**
   * High school transcripts (Grades 9–12) must be officially submitted.
   * Secure strong counselor and teacher recommendation letters.
   * Secure an official nomination from your school principal for the **UofT Lester B. Pearson Scholarship**.
